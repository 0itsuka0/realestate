Local social icon assets
=========================

All icon files in this directory are served from this site's own static files.
No social icon is fetched from a CDN or any third-party host at runtime.

Source notes:
- Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp, X, YouTube and Facebook path data: Simple Icons
  (https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), CC0 1.0.
- Aparat and Eitaa vector paths: aasaam/brand-icons
  (https://github.com/aasaam/brand-icons), MIT license.
- LinkedIn, Soroush, Bale, Rubika and Shad artwork in this sprite is locally
  drawn/assembled for this project. The names and marks remain trademarks of
  their respective owners; use them only to identify the corresponding links.
